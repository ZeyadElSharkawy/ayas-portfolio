Aya Hafez portfolio: sketches

Greyscale wireframes from the design discussion of 30 October 2026. Quiet, editorial, aimed at internship recruiters.

  1. 01Depth model
    Index, project and process layers, with CV and contact always one click away.
  2. 02Concept A: contact sheet
    Every project is a row of thumbnails; a row expands into its process chapters.
  3. 03Concept B: the rail
    Every look hangs on one clothing rail, grouped by project and filterable by discipline.
  4. 04Concept C: index and folio chosen
    A large serif contents list beside the work, with an outcome and process switch.
  5. 05Project page
    Hero, facts, outcome gallery, then five process chapters with a sticky chapter list.
